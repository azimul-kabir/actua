Actua is an unofficial, open-source native Android client for Actual Budget. It brings a mobile-first budgeting experience to Android with a modern Material 3 interface.

WHAT YOU CAN DO
• View budgets and track category progress
• Add, edit, search, and filter transactions
• Split transactions, make transfers, and reconcile accounts
• Group and reorder accounts, and manage categories, targets, and budget templates
• Track loans with a payoff planner, and see goal and target progress on your balances
• View scheduled transactions and a bills calendar, with optional reminders before they are due
• Review dashboards and reports, tap any chart to see its transactions, or open a report full screen with a data table
• Sync with your Actual Budget server and use supported bank-sync providers through that server
• Import transactions from CSV, OFX, QFX, QIF, CAMT.053, XLSX, PDF, SMS, and supported bank notifications, and review captured transactions before they are saved
• Queue transactions from Tasker and other automation apps
• Use automatic categorization and Actual Budget rules
• Create local backups and restore your budget data, or back up to your own Google Drive with encryption
• Budget on your device without a server, or import an Actual Budget archive
• Use home-screen widgets and launcher shortcuts
• Use Actua in English or Brazilian Portuguese

MADE FOR ANDROID
Actua is built as a native Android app, not a website wrapped in an app. Its Material 3 interface is designed for Android phones and supports features such as offline budgets, customizable navigation, and flexible appearance settings.

YOUR DATA, YOUR SERVER
Actua connects directly to your own Actual Budget server and keeps a local copy of your budget for offline use. It does not require a separate Actua account. You remain in control of your budget through Actual Budget and its supported synchronization methods.

New to Actua? You can explore the built-in local demo budget or create a budget on your device without connecting a server.

FREE AND OPEN SOURCE
Actua is free and open source. You can review the source code, report bugs, suggest improvements, or contribute to the project:
https://github.com/azimul-kabir/actua

UNOFFICIAL PROJECT
Actua is an independent community project. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, sponsored by, or an official product of Actual Budget. Actual Budget is a separate project; its name is used only to identify compatibility.

IMPORTANT
Actua requires access to your own Actual Budget server for connected budgeting features. Actua can write changes to your synchronized budget. Back up your important budget data before connecting a new client, and report bugs or unexpected synchronization behavior through the project's issue tracker.
