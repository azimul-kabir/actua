Manage your Actual Budget on Android. See what’s left to budget, track spending, and stay ahead of upcoming bills. Connect to your own Actual Budget server or create a standalone budget that works entirely offline.

MAKE YOUR BUDGET YOUR OWN

Customize Home to see Ready to Budget, favorite categories, upcoming bills, and recent activity at a glance. Track category progress, set targets, use budget templates, and organize your accounts.

KEEP TRANSACTIONS ORGANIZED

▶ Add, edit, search, and filter transactions.
▶ Scan receipts to fill in transaction details and keep the photos on your phone.
▶ Split transactions, make transfers, and reconcile accounts.
▶ Categorize transactions automatically with Actual Budget rules, or queue transactions from Tasker and other automation apps.

PLAN FOR BILLS AND LOANS

See scheduled transactions in a bills calendar and turn on reminders before payments are due. Set your own weekend days and holidays for pay periods and schedules. Track loans with a payoff planner for weekly, biweekly, or monthly payments, and see how your balances measure up against your goals.

SEE WHERE YOUR MONEY GOES

Explore dashboards and spending reports. Tap a chart to see the transactions behind it, or open a full-screen report with a data table.

IMPORT, EXPORT, AND SYNC

▶ Share CSV, OFX, QFX, QIF, CAMT.053, XLSX, and PDF statements directly into Actua.
▶ Capture transactions from SMS and supported bank notifications, then review them before saving.
▶ Export an account, a filtered transaction list, or search results to CSV.
▶ Use supported bank connections through your Actual Budget server, including SimpleFIN, GoCardless, Pluggy.ai, Akahu, and Enable Banking.

MADE FOR ANDROID

Actua is a native Android app with a Material 3 interface, customizable navigation, and flexible appearance settings. Add home-screen widgets for your budget, favorite categories, balances, bills, spending trends, and reports, or jump in with launcher shortcuts. Available in English, French, German, Italian, Japanese, Russian, Simplified Chinese, Brazilian Portuguese, and Spanish.

YOUR DATA, YOUR SERVER

Actua connects directly to your Actual Budget server and keeps a local copy for offline use. No separate Actua account is required. To try Actua without a server, open the built-in demo, create an on-device budget, or import an Actual Budget archive.

Create and restore local backups, or save encrypted backups to your own Google Drive or WebDAV server. Lock Actua with biometrics or your device PIN and hide it from the Android recents screen.

ABOUT THIS PROJECT

Actua is an independent, unofficial client for Actual Budget. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by the Actual Budget project.

Connected budgeting requires access to your own Actual Budget server. Actua can write changes to your synced budget, so back up important data before connecting a new client.
