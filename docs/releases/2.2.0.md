# Actua 2.2.0

Actua 2.2.0 lets pay periods and schedules follow your own working week: choose your weekend days, move
the month start day to the previous or next working day, list holidays to skip, and see weekend-skipping
schedules, and post them, on the dates that match. It also adds a target funding icon to Budget table
balance pills and fixes three bugs.

## Before you update

- Weekend days, the working-day adjustment and the holiday list are **device-local**. They change what
  Actua shows on this device only; Actual and your other devices keep their own dates, and nothing about
  them is synced.
- By default nothing changes: Saturday and Sunday stay the weekend, the month start day keeps its day, and
  there are no holidays.
- A schedule's date in Actual does not change. Actua works out the date it shows from Actual's date, so
  another device or Actual itself still shows the original one.
- As always, let Actua finish syncing before you update, and keep an independent backup from Actual before
  using a new version with a real budget.

## Added

### Weekend days, working days and holidays

- Weekend days and a previous or next working day option for the month start day in Settings > Budget, so a
  pay period that starts on the 25th starts on the Thursday when the 25th is a Friday or Saturday ([#1294](https://github.com/azimul-kabir/actua/pull/1294))
- Schedules that skip weekends are shown on your chosen weekend days in Schedules, Home, upcoming rows, the
  bills calendar, the schedule widget and reminders ([#1295](https://github.com/azimul-kabir/actua/pull/1295))
- Posting such a schedule dates the transaction on the date shown, as long as the schedule still reads as
  paid; exact-date and auto-posting schedules keep Actual's date ([#1298](https://github.com/azimul-kabir/actua/pull/1298))
- A holiday list in Settings > Budget > Holidays that the working-day month start and weekend-skipping
  schedules skip like weekend days ([#1299](https://github.com/azimul-kabir/actua/pull/1299))

### Budget

- The target funding icon on balance pills in the Budget table view ([#1289](https://github.com/azimul-kabir/actua/pull/1289))

## Fixed

- The Sankey card ignored the month start day when its time frame was the current month, last month or a
  sliding window ([#1292](https://github.com/azimul-kabir/actua/pull/1292))
- OpenID sign-in hung in the browser when it opened an idle connection to Actua first ([#1285](https://github.com/azimul-kabir/actua/pull/1285))
- Goal categories ignored the Goal in progress and Goal reached status colors on the dot and balance pill ([#1288](https://github.com/azimul-kabir/actua/pull/1288))

## Compatibility and safety

- Weekend days, the working-day adjustment and holidays are stored in Android `SharedPreferences` per
  budget and are never written to the budget file or synced. Actual has no equivalent setting.
- A schedule's synced `next_date` and the post, skip and complete actions stay Actual's own Saturday/Sunday
  results. The only value that follows your weekend is the date of a transaction you post, and only when it
  still counts as paying that occurrence.
- No database schema, Merkle or sync-protocol changes.
- The main navigation host was restructured so its code stays under the JVM's method size limit; the
  screens and their behavior are unchanged.
