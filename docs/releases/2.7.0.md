# Actua 2.7.0

Actua 2.7.0 adds five home-screen widgets and refreshes the rest, makes Home's sections collapsible, and gives the tab
bar a translucent look with an optional blur. Screens load their data in the background, and going back or switching
tabs keeps your place. In the Google Play version you can now support Actua monthly, and Actua Supporters get optional
cosmetic extras. Budgeting itself always stays free.

## Before you update

- As always, let Actua finish syncing before you update, and keep an independent backup from Actual before using a
  new version with a real budget.

  schedules ([#1631](https://github.com/azimul-kabir/actua/pull/1631))

## Notes

- **Supporter extras are cosmetic and optional.** They exist only in the Google Play version, and budgeting stays free
  for everyone. Supporter status is checked with Google Play and cached on the device; it is never sent anywhere else.
  If it lapses, every extra returns to its default. The F-Droid build, which is also the main `Actua-v2.7.0.apk` on
  GitHub, has no Supporter extras, no support invitation and no rating request.
- **Going back keeps your place.** Back from Credit cards, Loans or Payee locations returns to the Settings page you
  opened them from, the back gesture now goes where the on-screen arrow goes, each Settings page keeps its own scroll
  position, and switching tabs no longer resets a tab you re-tapped earlier. Re-tapping the current tab still takes you
  to its top or root.
- **The tab bar blur is a setting.** It is on by default on Android 12 and newer. Turning it off in Settings → Display
  makes the tab bar and the Transaction button opaque.
