# Actua 2.5.1

Actua 2.5.1 fixes Scan receipt, which failed with "Could not read that image" on every photo in 2.5.0. It also
fixes two Credit Cards screen mistakes and adds Spanish as a display language.

## Before you update

- Receipt scanning is in the Google Play version and the `-full` APK only; this fix does not change that.
- As always, let Actua finish syncing before you update, and keep an independent backup from Actual before using a
  new version with a real budget.

## Added

- Added Spanish as a display language ([#1447](https://github.com/azimul-kabir/actua/pull/1447)), thanks to
  [@stuffsearcher](https://github.com/stuffsearcher)

## Changed

- Made receipt scanning load photos more safely, and a failed scan now names its error and appears in Diagnostics
  ([#1486](https://github.com/azimul-kabir/actua/pull/1486))
- The privacy policy now says that receipt scanning's Google ML Kit may send technical diagnostics to Google
  ([#1482](https://github.com/azimul-kabir/actua/pull/1482))

## Fixed

- Fixed Scan receipt failing with "Could not read that image" on every photo
  ([#1493](https://github.com/azimul-kabir/actua/pull/1493))
- Fixed the Credit Cards screen hiding an unpaid statement that is past its due date
  ([#1491](https://github.com/azimul-kabir/actua/pull/1491))
- Fixed the Credit Cards screen showing open-cycle spending as due
  ([#1489](https://github.com/azimul-kabir/actua/pull/1489))
