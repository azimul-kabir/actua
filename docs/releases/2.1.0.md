# Actua 2.1.0

Actua 2.1.0 adds loan tracking with a payoff planner, a floating tab bar that gets out of the way
while you scroll, a month start day for people who are not paid on the 1st, goal and target icons on
Budget balance pills, and a Brazilian Portuguese translation with in-app language selection.

## Before you update

- The month start day is **device-local**. It changes how Actua groups months on this device only;
  Actual and your other devices keep showing calendar months, and no saved report is rewritten.
- The tab bar now hides while you scroll down by default. Turn it off under Settings > Display >
  "Hide tab bar while scrolling".
- As always, let Actua finish syncing before you update, and keep an independent backup from Actual
  before using a new version with a real budget.

## Added

### Loans

- Loans screen in Settings to track a loan account's balance, interest rate, payment and payoff progress ([#1255](https://github.com/azimul-kabir/actua/pull/1255))
- Projected payoff month for each loan ([#1256](https://github.com/azimul-kabir/actua/pull/1256))
- Loan card on loan accounts with payoff progress, payment schedule and projected payoff ([#1257](https://github.com/azimul-kabir/actua/pull/1257))
- Record loan payments split into interest, escrow and a categorized transfer, and pair a loan with a budget category ([#1258](https://github.com/azimul-kabir/actua/pull/1258))
- Payoff planner with a burndown chart and a total-paid figure ([#1262](https://github.com/azimul-kabir/actua/pull/1262))
- The paired category's monthly target on the loan card, with a per-month skip so budget template runs leave that category alone ([#1263](https://github.com/azimul-kabir/actua/pull/1263))

### Credit cards

- Billing-cycle card separates the last statement from the current cycle and shows previous-cycle spend and credit utilization ([#1245](https://github.com/azimul-kabir/actua/pull/1245))
- Pay statement button that opens a prefilled transfer for the amount due ([#1247](https://github.com/azimul-kabir/actua/pull/1247))

### Navigation and Budget

- The bottom tab bar is a floating pill, keeping your tab order, the Add tab and the labels setting ([#1265](https://github.com/azimul-kabir/actua/pull/1265))
- The tab bar slides away while you scroll down and returns when you scroll up, with a switch in Settings > Display ([#1269](https://github.com/azimul-kabir/actua/pull/1269))
- Fully rounded Budget balance pills that show an icon when a category has a target ([#1280](https://github.com/azimul-kabir/actua/pull/1280))
- Balance pills show how far a goal, save-by-date or cover-schedule target is funded: not started, in progress, reached or funded past it ([#1281](https://github.com/azimul-kabir/actua/pull/1281))

### Reports and month start day

- Custom report month presets (This month, Last month, Last N months) follow the month start day, so pay-period pie charts work ([#1270](https://github.com/azimul-kabir/actua/pull/1270))
- Summary and Cash Flow cards default to the current pay period ([#1272](https://github.com/azimul-kabir/actua/pull/1272))
- The Sankey card defaults to the last 6 pay periods ([#1273](https://github.com/azimul-kabir/actua/pull/1273))

## Compatibility and safety

- Loan terms are stored in Actual's synced `preferences` table under `actuali:loan:<accountId>`, using
  Actuali's JSON fields plus a few optional keys Actuali ignores. Recording a payment writes its
  interest/escrow rows and transfer through Actual's CRDT message path as one atomic insert.
- The month start day, the tab bar behavior and the pill icons are device-local presentation. Nothing
  about them is synced, and stored report and widget settings are never rewritten, so Actual keeps
  showing calendar months.
- No database schema, Merkle or sync-protocol changes.
