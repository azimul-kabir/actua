# Actua 1.8.0

Actua 1.8.0 is the first release for Google Play's production track. It makes captured
transactions much faster to review: from bank notifications, shared text and Tasker. It also
marks what bank sync just added, adds reminders before scheduled transactions are due, and adds
Actual's Make transfer, keypad arithmetic and currency symbols after the amount.

## Before you update

- **Capturing stays opt-in.** Reading bank notifications needs both the capture switch and
  Android's notification access for Actua, and only the apps you pick are read. Captured
  transactions wait in a review queue on the device; nothing is written to your budget until you
  approve it. See [PRIVACY.md](https://github.com/azimul-kabir/actua/blob/main/PRIVACY.md#bank-notifications-and-automation-apps).
- **Notifications are optional.** Schedule reminders and capture notifications are off by
  default. Android 13 and later ask for notification permission when you turn either on.
- **Android 17 asks for Nearby devices access only for local servers.** The permission is
  requested only when your Actual server's address is on your local network, and the Connection
  screen explains why.
- As always, let Actua finish syncing before you update, and keep an independent backup from
  Actual before using a new version with a real budget.

## Added

### Captured transactions

- Captured transactions can use a default account, and Review captured sets an account for all rows at once ([#1067](https://github.com/azimul-kabir/actua/pull/1067))
- A Review captured launcher shortcut, and the Accounts tab icon counts waiting captures from any tab ([#1068](https://github.com/azimul-kabir/actua/pull/1068))
- The Accounts tab shows how many captured transactions are waiting, and opens them on their own review page ([#1052](https://github.com/azimul-kabir/actua/pull/1052))
- Captured transactions can post a notification to review them or import them with one tap ([#1049](https://github.com/azimul-kabir/actua/pull/1049))
- Captured transactions start with the account you last chose for the same card or app ([#1048](https://github.com/azimul-kabir/actua/pull/1048))
- Captured transactions each have their own account, so a queue for several accounts imports in one go ([#1038](https://github.com/azimul-kabir/actua/pull/1038))

### Tasker and automation

- A Tasker plugin action, Queue transaction, sends transactions to the review queue from a ready-made form ([#1041](https://github.com/azimul-kabir/actua/pull/1041))
- The Tasker plugin form lets you pick the account from a list ([#1043](https://github.com/azimul-kabir/actua/pull/1043))
- The import screen shows why the last Tasker broadcast was ignored ([#1035](https://github.com/azimul-kabir/actua/pull/1035))

### Bank sync, schedules and transactions

- Transactions added by bank sync are marked as new on their account until you view it ([#1069](https://github.com/azimul-kabir/actua/pull/1069))
- Optional reminders before scheduled transactions are due ([#1055](https://github.com/azimul-kabir/actua/pull/1055))
- Make transfer links two selected transactions in different accounts as one transfer, as in Actual ([#1036](https://github.com/azimul-kabir/actua/pull/1036))
- The amount keypad has multiply, divide and equals keys ([#1039](https://github.com/azimul-kabir/actua/pull/1039))
- Currency symbols can go after the amount, with or without a space, as in Actual ([#1054](https://github.com/azimul-kabir/actua/pull/1054))

## Compatibility and safety

- Captured, shared and Tasker transactions are never written directly: they are imported only
  from the review page or a notification's Import action, through the same atomic CRDT import
  path as statement files.
- The capture queue, remembered and default capture accounts, the Tasker token and bank-sync
  "new" marks are device-local, kept per budget where they name accounts, and never synced. Bank
  sync records only the ids of the rows it added; matching, updating and writing are unchanged.
- Make transfer follows Actual's `validForTransfer` checks and links both legs in one CRDT batch.
- Schedule reminders are planned on the device from the open budget's schedules and replanned
  after local writes and syncs; they never change a schedule.
- Keypad arithmetic and currency symbol position change entry and presentation only; stored
  amounts stay integer cents.
- No database schema, encryption, Merkle or sync-protocol changes.
